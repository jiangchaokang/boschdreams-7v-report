    Each ticket becomes a world.

    The fleet reports a failure. An AI agent reads it, searches the recorded drives for a road that fits, edits it in the vector world, checks the physics, and has BoschDreams-7V film it with all seven cameras. Every world ships with a ground-truth trajectory, so it can train and test the planner with no person in the loop.

                One decision, many futures.

                Closed-loop simulation. Every future starts from the same recorded frame and is one drive until the decision. Then the car commits, the traffic reacts by rule, and BoschDreams-7V renders what the cameras see.

                  Scope

                  • Real time at 480p.480p 1-step streams at 10.85 FPS per camera; 720p 4-step is the offline, high-fidelity setting.
                  • Rare physics.Contact and far out-of-distribution interactions can still show artifacts.
                  • Rule-based traffic.Reactions come from the vector simulator (IDM, yielding), not learned drivers.
                  • Complements the road.It widens closed-loop and corner-case coverage; it does not replace road validation.
